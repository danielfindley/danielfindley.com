Bench

The first eleven of your round picks that make a legal shape (1 GK, 3–5 D, 2–5 M, 1–3 F), taken in draft order and filled in as you go. Anyone who would break the shape, or arrives once a position is full, drops to the bench.

Use the ⋯ options in the table above to pencil a player in; you pick the round (and pick vs candidate) in the follow-up menu. Drag a chip onto empty space in another round to move it there, or drop it onto another chip to take that slot: drop a candidate on the pick and it becomes the pick, with the old one demoted to candidate. A player lives in one round at a time. The highlighted pick chip is what the squad-shape pills count; everything after the divider is a backup candidate; clicking a candidate also promotes it. Copy duplicates the current plan so you can try a variation without losing the original. Set my slot and each round shows your overall pick number (12-team snake); a chip's #ADP-rank turns amber when it's earlier than that pick; they may not make it back to you. Crossed-out players show struck here. Saved in this browser.

Position Tiers

Tier players from the board's ⋯ options → Add to position tier; the position bucket comes from the player, so a midfielder put in tier 1 lands in M1. Each row is coloured by its tier, and within a tier players are ordered by ADP. Use ▲ ▼ to shuffle someone between tiers and ✕ to untier them. Each row has the ⋯ options menu too, so you can cross a player out, pencil them into a plan round or move their tier without going back up to the board. Tier badges also show next to the player's name everywhere else in the app.

How to read it: ADP (average draft pick, from mock drafts) is the default sort; Rd = ADP as a 12-team round; PtsRk = last season's total points rank (hover the headers for details); a PtsRk much lower than ADP is a value pick. Positions are next season's Fantrax classifications. Goalkeepers show real points only (FP/90 via FotMob minutes); Championship players carry ADP only. xFP/90 is Fantrax points per 90 with actual goals and assists replaced by xG and xA (goal = 10/9/9 pts and assist = 8/6/6 pts for D/M/F). Luck = FPts − xFP: + hot finished above expected (regression risk), − cold finished below it (buy-low candidate). + adds straight to your active ranking (the numbered badge shows their spot; click it to remove); ⋯ opens options: cross out as drafted, rank, jump to an exact ranking spot, pencil into a plan round (you pick the round in the follow-up), toggle any of your lists, or remove a player who has left the league. ＋ New player adds someone who isn't in the data yet (incoming transfer): pick their club and optionally an estimated ADP; they slot into the ADP order by it (below your ranked players), or sit at the top of the ADP section without one. Click a player's name for season-by-season history back to 2019/20. Search matches players and clubs (code, full name, or "spurs" / "man utd"); when the text names a club, pick it from the suggestions to filter the board to that squad. Use ★ ⚑ ▲ ▼ for your own tags. The board leads with your active ranking, then the rest by ADP. Click any column to sort; sort ranked decides what that does: leave it off and your ranked players stay on top in your ranking order while only the pool below them sorts by that column; tick it and the whole list sorts together. A third click on the header goes back to ranking order. The stats control picks which stat columns are shown (applies to every table). Everything saves in this browser.

Set-piece takers

Projected 2026/27 duties from the Solio Analytics model (@robtfpl). Names read left to right in priority order: first is first choice. Tap a name to look them up on the board.

Set-piece duty is one of the few genuinely predictable sources of points: the corner taker collects key passes and assists every week, and the penalty taker gets goals that have nothing to do with open play. A second name means a shared or contested duty, so treat those as less certain than a lone name.
