Rounds

Use the ⋯ options in the table above to pencil a player in; you pick the round (and pick vs candidate) in the follow-up menu; drag chips between rounds to move them. A player lives in one round at a time. The highlighted pick chip is what the squad-shape pills count; everything after the divider is a backup candidate; click a candidate to make it the pick. Set my slot and each round shows your overall pick number (12-team snake); a chip's #ADP-rank turns amber when it's earlier than that pick; they may not make it back to you. Crossed-out players show struck here. Saved in this browser.

Add players from the table's ⋯ options → Lists menu; toggle them in or out of any list. Each list card is a full-stat sortable table with its own search-to-add.

How to read it: ADP (average draft pick, from mock drafts) is the default sort; Rd = ADP as a 12-team round; PtsRk = last season's total points rank (hover the headers for details); a PtsRk much lower than ADP is a value pick. Positions are next season's Fantrax classifications. Goalkeepers show real points only (FP/90 via FotMob minutes); Championship players carry ADP only. xFP/90 is Fantrax points per 90 with actual goals and assists replaced by xG and xA (goal = 10/9/9 pts and assist = 8/6/6 pts for D/M/F). Luck = FPts − xFP: + hot finished above expected (regression risk), − cold finished below it (buy-low candidate). + adds straight to your active ranking (the numbered badge shows their spot; click it to remove); ⋯ opens options: cross out as drafted, rank, jump to an exact ranking spot, pencil into a plan round (you pick the round in the follow-up), toggle any of your lists, or remove a player who has left the league. ＋ New player adds someone who isn't in the data yet (incoming transfer): pick their club and optionally an estimated ADP; they slot into the ADP order by it (below your ranked players), or sit at the top of the ADP section without one. Click a player's name for season-by-season history back to 2019/20. Use ★ ⚑ ▲ ▼ for your own tags. Click any column to sort; the order control sorts by one of your rankings (ranked first, rest by ADP); the stats control picks which stat columns are shown (applies to every table). Everything saves in this browser.
