MLS Percentile Rankings

2026 Season · 300+ Minutes · Per 90 Stats
Every MLS player with 300+ minutes in the 2026 season is ranked against others at their position. Players are grouped into six comparison pools — goalkeepers, centre-backs, fullbacks/wingbacks, midfielders, attacking midfielders/wingers, and strikers — so a centre-back's tackles are measured against other centre-backs, not forwards. All counting stats are normalized to per-90-minute rates to account for differences in playing time. The percentile bar shows where a player sits relative to everyone else in their group: green (top third), gray (middle), red (bottom third).
